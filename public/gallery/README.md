# Practice photographs

Drop image files in this folder and they appear in the gallery automatically —
on /gallery in full, and the first six on the home page. No code change needed.
While this folder holds no images, the gallery falls back to stock photography.

## Naming

The filename becomes the caption, and optionally the category:

    01-facility--LMCH-surgical-department.jpg   ->  Facility / LMCH Surgical Department
    02-events--metabolic-surgery-symposium.jpg  ->  Events   / Metabolic Surgery Symposium
    03-consultation-room.jpg                    ->  Practice / Consultation Room

- The leading number only fixes the running order.
- Everything before `--` is the category; everything after is the caption.
- Words of five characters or fewer in CAPITALS stay capitalised (FMH, LMCH, MGB).
- Accepted extensions: .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .avif

## Before adding a photograph

- **Patients**: do not publish any image in which a patient is identifiable
  without their written consent.
- **Colleagues and students**: the teaching and conference photographs contain
  identifiable people. Get their agreement before they go on a commercial site.
- **Size**: keep files under roughly 500 KB. These are served unoptimised, so a
  4 MB phone photograph will slow the page down noticeably.
